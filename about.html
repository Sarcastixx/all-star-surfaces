About us

A Lynnwood shop for the floors we install.

ALLSTAR Surfaces is the showroom and the crew. You see the material here. The same people come back to put it down.

Who we are

Samples in daylight. Then a written scope.

The lines we install sit in the shop at 18000 Hwy 99, so the grain is judged in real light before anything is ordered. We measure the rooms, check the subfloor, and leave a scope you can read. Founded in 2007.

Homes around Seattle, the Eastside, and the I-5 corridor. Most residential installs take 3–10 days once the material is in.

What we install

Floors, and the rooms around them.

Hardwood, stone, tile, luxury vinyl, and carpet. Stairs when they have to match the field. Refinishing when the floor that is already there is worth keeping.
